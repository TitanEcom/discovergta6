Who we are

About GTA Wiki

GTA Wiki is an unofficial fan archive about Grand Theft Auto VI. It collects what Rockstar has shown and said about GTA 6, in plain English, with a source behind every entry.

What this site is

GTA Wiki lives at discovergta6.com and covers one game: Grand Theft Auto VI. It has a page for every named character, location, business, vehicle and weapon, radio station, gameplay system and edition, plus a news timeline from the 2022 confirmation to release day.

It is a fan project with no legal entity behind it. It is not made, endorsed or controlled by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony or Microsoft, and it sells nothing.

How entries are written

Every entry is paraphrased from public material: Rockstar’s own GTA VI pages and trailers, Rockstar Support articles, Take-Two press releases and trade reporting. The full list is on the sources page. The archive is currently updated through October 25, 2026.

  • No leaks. Leaked builds and rumors are not a source. If something is only reported and not confirmed by Rockstar, the entry says so.
  • Status labels. Each entry carries a status: confirmed by Rockstar, edition content, preview, background or still unconfirmed.
  • Primary text wins. If Rockstar and a guide disagree, the entry follows Rockstar and notes the difference.
  • No played copy yet. Gameplay pages describe announcements and trailers, not hands-on play.

Corrections

Every entry has a “Send a correction” link. You can also use the contact page to report a mistake or suggest a missing entry. Questions about orders, codes or the game itself go to Rockstar Support.
