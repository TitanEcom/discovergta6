About Discover GTA 6

Discover GTA 6 is an independent fan guide to Grand Theft Auto VI. It collects everything Rockstar has shown and said about the game, in plain English, with a source behind every entry.

Who we are

Discover GTA 6 is run by an independent GTA fan based in the Netherlands. The site exists to keep every announcement about GTA 6 in one place, so you do not have to piece it together from trailers, store pages, and press coverage.

It is a fan project. It is not made, endorsed, or controlled by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony, or Microsoft, and it does not sell the game.

What you will find here

  • A countdown to November 19, 2026 with the key facts about release, preload, price, and platforms.
  • A page for every named character, location, business, vehicle and weapon, and radio station.
  • Explanations of gameplay features, a comparison of the editions, and a news timeline from 2022 to release day.
  • A schematic map of Leonida and every official trailer.

How we write

Every entry is paraphrased from public material: Rockstar’s own GTA VI pages and trailers, Rockstar Support articles, Take-Two press releases, and trade reporting. Each page lists its sources, and the full overview is on the sources page.

  • No leaks. Leaked builds and rumors are not a source. If something is only reported and not confirmed by Rockstar, the entry says so.
  • Status labels. Each entry shows whether it is confirmed by Rockstar, edition content, a preview, background, or still unconfirmed.
  • Primary text wins. If Rockstar and a guide disagree, the entry follows Rockstar and notes the difference.
  • No played copy yet. Until release, gameplay pages describe announcements and trailers, not hands-on play.

How the site is funded

Discover GTA 6 is free to read. To cover hosting and the time it takes to keep the guide up to date, the site shows ads through Google AdSense. Ads appear alongside the content and are kept separate from it. The privacy page explains how ads and analytics use cookies.

Contact and corrections

Spotted a mistake or missing an entry? Every page has a “Send a correction” link, and you can reach us any time through the contact form. Questions about orders, codes, or the game itself go to Rockstar Support.

Other projects

The people behind Discover GTA 6 run a few other sites as well. They are listed on the sitelinks page.
