Vintage Vice City Pack

Stanier, a garage, a retro look, a weapon pattern, and a month of GTA+ with a timely digital purchase.

The Vintage Vice City Pack looks back at the neon of the old Vice City, including a nod to Tommy Vercetti. Rockstar Support names the '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern.

A month of GTA+ is included. On PlayStation, the store text says it has to be claimed before March 31, 2027, after which the membership continues monthly until you cancel. Other platforms can have different fine print.

The physical box contains a download code and no disc. Those boxes are in stores from November 12 so that preload is possible. Some retailers were publicly critical of that.

Rockstar's homepage sums it up: pre-order 'to get unique benefits that flash back to when the neon burned brightest.' The pack comes with both the Standard and the Ultimate Edition.
