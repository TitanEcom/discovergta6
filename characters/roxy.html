Roxy

One of the two rappers in Real Dimez.

Roxy is the other half of Real Dimez, alongside Bae-Luxe. Like her partner, she has no solo profile: Rockstar describes the duo as a unit.

Their official story starts in high school and runs through shaking down local dealers, viral rap tracks, and an early hit with local rapper DWNPLY. Five years later they are signed to Only Raw Records, hoping for a second hit.

In one official screenshot, Roxy rides in the passenger seat next to Dre'Quan Priest. Coverage of the images mentions Stock 305 streetwear and custom chains in her wardrobe as well. Rockstar has not named a voice actor.
