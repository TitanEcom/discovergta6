Characters

Lucia Caminos

Playable lead. Just out of Leonida Penitentiary, and the first female protagonist in the series who is not optional.

Lucia Caminos is the other playable lead. Her father taught her to fight early. A conflict over her family put her in Leonida's state prison. She gets out and wants the life her mother had pictured since their years around Liberty City, this time on her own terms.

She is the first female protagonist in a main game who is not optional. Earlier games did have female avatars, including the first game, the Game Boy Color version of the second, and GTA Online, but those were silent or interchangeable with a male avatar.

The Ultimate Edition gives her an engraved Klose K17 of her own and, with Jason, a pair of Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers.
