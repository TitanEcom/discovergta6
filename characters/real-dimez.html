Real Dimez

Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to Only Raw Records.

Real Dimez is a rap duo: Bae-Luxe and Roxy. Rockstar presents them as artists on Only Raw, the label of Dre'Quan Priest and Boobie Ike.

Beyond that place in the cast, no tracklist or biography has been released. The October 2026 radio preview is a different selection, and it is not an album by this duo.

According to Rockstar, Bae-Luxe and Roxy have been friends since high school. They turned their time shaking down local dealers into cash through 'spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence.' The profile tagline reads 'Viral videos. Viral hooks.'

An early hit single with local rapper DWNPLY took them to new heights. Five years and 'a whole lot of trouble' later, they are signed to Only Raw Records, hoping lightning can strike twice.
