Characters

Jason Duval

Playable lead. A former soldier who works for smugglers in the Leonida Keys.

Jason Duval is one of the two characters you control. Rockstar sketches a man who grew up among small-time criminals, spent a stretch in the army trying to leave that childhood behind, and then ended up with local smugglers in the Keys anyway.

He rents a place from Brian Heder, an old hand in that same smuggling world. Cal Hampton, a paranoid friend, moves in the same circles. Meeting Lucia could be a way out for Jason, or the moment everything turns.

In the Ultimate Edition, his safehouse includes a Dinka Enduro in army colors and a Crest kayak. His beat-up Vapid Ganado picks up extra retro parts there.
