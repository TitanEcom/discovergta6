Frequently asked questions

What do the status labels mean?

Confirmed means Rockstar said or showed it. Edition means it is in an official edition text. Preview means it was shown before launch and may change. Unconfirmed means it comes from reports only.

How often is this wiki updated?

Whenever Rockstar shares something new, and at least every few days in the run-up to launch. Each page shows how recent its information is.
