Frequently asked questions

All questions
When does GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 comes out on Thursday, November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has held that date since November 2025, after two delays. More about release day.

Is GTA 6 out yet?

Not yet. GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026. Digital pre-orders and boxed codes can be preloaded from November 12, so you can install it a week early. How preload works.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US, the Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99. The price is the same on PS5 and Xbox. Local store prices can differ by country.

Can you play GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. GTA 6 is only announced for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC version. A GamesBeat report from October 2026 points to early 2027, but that is unconfirmed. PC status.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In the fictional state of Leonida, Rockstar's version of Florida. The main city is Vice City, based on Miami, with five more regions around it. See the map.
