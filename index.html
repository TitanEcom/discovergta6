GTA 6 release date and key facts

Discover GTA 6 is an independent fan guide to the game. Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) comes out on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar moved the date twice before settling on this one. Here is what is confirmed so far, summarized from official announcements through October 25, 2026.

  • Release date: November 19, 2026. Why the date moved.
  • Preload: digital pre-orders can download from November 12, 2026 at local midnight. The physical edition is a code in a box, not a disc. Preload details.
  • PC: there is no PC version on release day, and Rockstar has not announced one. PC status.
  • Price: Standard Edition $79.99, Ultimate Edition $99.99 (US list prices). Compare the editions.
  • Pre-order bonus: the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ with a digital purchase before November 20, 2026. What is in the pack.
  • Online: Rockstar calls the game a single-player experience at launch. Single-player details.
  • Setting and leads: the state of Leonida and Vice City, with Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as the two playable characters. See the map.
