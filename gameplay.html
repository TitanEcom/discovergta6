The game

GTA 6 Gameplay

A third-person action-adventure in an open Leonida. What follows comes from announcements and the August 2026 Extended Look, not from a played copy.

What you do

You play as Jason or Lucia. The freedom is the series’ freedom: take vehicles, rob businesses, and drive off when the police show up. The Extended Look added skydiving, wrestling, basketball, working out, and diving. Fishing is named in the Squalo text.

You can keep the pair together or split them up. Their bond shifts with how you play, and the studio called that relationship optional. Safehouses across the state have been compared in the press to the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2.

Body and reputation

Both characters can gain or lose weight. Days without sleep show in the face. Hair, clothes, and accessories are yours to change. Criminal Profile tracks whether a crime stays businesslike or turns needlessly violent. It is related to Red Dead’s honor system, and it is explicitly not a path that turns them into heroes.

A test in which you bought groceries and stocked the fridge was cut. The studio found it slowed the game down.

The world around them

Snapmatic returns on the phone, as a satire of photo apps and short video. The same phone carries the radio outside the car. Weather can rain locally, leave puddles, and build into a hurricane. Residents vary more in body type and react to eavesdropping or a drawn weapon.

Launch specs

The game runs on RAGE, built for this console generation. Both machines target 30 frames per second. There is no PC version on release day. A date in the first quarter of 2027 is circulating and is not confirmed.
