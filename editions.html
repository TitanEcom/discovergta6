GTA 6 Editions

Prices are the US list prices from Take-Two’s press release. Local store prices on PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store can differ. This site sells nothing.

Standard Edition

$79.99

  • The full single-player game
  • Vintage Vice City Pack with a digital purchase before November 20, 2026
  • One month of GTA+ with that same purchase
  • Ultimate later as a separate upgrade

Ultimate Edition

$99.99

  • Everything in Standard
  • Cheetah, Squalo, buggy, Enduro, kayak, Ganado rebuild
  • Morgan revolvers and engraved pistols
  • Style, tattoos, Stockyard shops, PTT raid, classic-car mission

Vintage Vice City Pack

A digital pre-order or purchase of either edition before November 20, 2026 includes a ’55 Vapid Stanier with a garage, retro outfits and hair, an exclusive weapon pattern, and one month of GTA+. On PlayStation, the store text says that month must be claimed before March 31, 2027. After that the membership continues until you cancel. The Ultimate upgrade on its own does not qualify again.

Preload

Downloads start at local midnight on November 12, 2026. The physical box is in stores from that day and contains a code, not a disc. The game itself unlocks on November 19.

What is not included

Rockstar told the press that neither version has microtransactions in the game at launch, and that the game does not use generative AI. The official page does show an in-game purchases label. Read that next to GTA+, not as a confirmed store inside VI. A PC version is not part of these editions on release day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GTA 6 cost?

$79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US. Local prices can differ.

Is there a disc version of GTA 6?

No. The boxed version contains a download code, not a disc.

What is the pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack with the '55 Vapid Stanier, a garage, retro outfits, a weapon pattern, and a month of GTA+, for digital purchases before November 20, 2026.
