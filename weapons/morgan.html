Hawk & Little Morgan

His-and-hers revolvers with palm engraving, in the style of the Vercetti estate.

The Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers are a set for Jason and Lucia. The edition text mentions grips with palm trees, engraving, a scope, and a design in the style of the Vercetti estate, the reference to Tommy Vercetti from Vice City.

The Ultimate text: 'His and hers versions of this powerful revolver with classic Vice City stylings sourced from the Vercetti Estate, including palm-tree-etched grips, engraved detailing, and high-performance scope.' The text says the styling comes from the Vercetti Estate. It does not say the revolvers are found there.
