'95 Grotti Cheetah

A mid-1990s sports car from Grotti. It unlocks only later in the story.

The Cheetah is Grotti's sports car from the middle of the 1990s, with a plain retro-futuristic livery, described as an ode to Shore Drive. Rockstar says it shows up later in the action. It is not there on the first day of play.
