Shitzu Squalo

A pink-and-blue speedboat at Washington Beach, with a crate of explosives.

The Shitzu Squalo from the Ultimate Edition is a speedboat in a fade from pink to blue. It is moored at Washington Beach, meant for open water and Gambit Bay, and it has a weapons crate with explosives on board.
