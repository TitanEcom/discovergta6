'55 Vapid Stanier

A classic sedan from the Vintage Vice City Pack, including a garage of its own.

Anyone who orders or buys digitally before November 20, 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack. It includes a 1955 Vapid Stanier, plus a garage to keep it in.

Rockstar's support page names the garage without a neighborhood. Fan sites talk about Shore Court near Ocean Beach. That place name is listed here as a guide name, not as a quote from the support text.

Rockstar's homepage presents the pack as benefits that 'flash back to when the neon burned brightest.' Besides the Stanier, it includes retro outfits for Jason and Lucia and a weapon pattern.

The pack comes with both editions. If you buy the Standard Edition digitally in time, the Stanier and its garage are yours too.
