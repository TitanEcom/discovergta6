Garage

Vehicles and weapons

Only what received a name in the edition list. Trailers show many more cars, boats, and aircraft, without a catalog to go with them yet.

Vehicles

Vehicle · Edition '55 Vapid Stanier A classic sedan from the Vintage Vice City Pack, including a garage of its own. Vehicle · Edition '95 Grotti Cheetah A mid-1990s sports car from Grotti. It unlocks only later in the story. Vehicle · Edition Vapid Ganado Jason's worn-out pickup. The Ultimate Edition adds a retro rebuild. Vehicle · Edition Dinka Enduro A motorcycle in army colors, parked at Jason's safehouse. Vehicle · Edition Crest Kayak A kayak at Jason's safehouse, part of the Ultimate Edition. Vehicle · Edition Shitzu Squalo A pink-and-blue speedboat at Washington Beach, with a crate of explosives. Vehicle · Edition '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy A 1967 mud buggy, stored at Paradise Garage.

Weapons

Weapon · Edition Hawk & Little Morgan His-and-hers revolvers with palm engraving, in the style of the Vercetti estate. Weapon · Edition Girardi ES9 Jason's pistol. The Ultimate Edition adds an engraved version. Weapon · Edition Klose K17 Lucia's pistol, with an engraved Ultimate variant.
