Where it comes from

Sources

Everything is paraphrased through October 25, 2026. Where a detail exists only in a guide, the entry says so. Leaked builds are not a source.

  • Rockstar Games: Grand Theft Auto VI. Date, platforms, story setup, trailers, editions, and the launch trailer.
  • Rockstar Support: editions and preload. Stanier pack, GTA+, November 12, box without a disc.
  • Take-Two: pre-order release. June 25, 2026, $79.99 and $99.99, single-player.
  • Wikipedia: Grand Theft Auto VI. Credits, development, gameplay from the Extended Look, timeline. Used as a reference, not as text to copy.

Radio hosts, preview tracks, and part of the Ultimate list come from reporting around the official music page and the October 2026 store text. If Rockstar and a guide disagree, the primary text wins and the difference stays in the entry.
