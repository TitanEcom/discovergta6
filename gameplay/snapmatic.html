Snapmatic

The social channel from GTA V returns, as a satire of photo apps and short-video apps.

Snapmatic is on the phone again. This time it is written as a parody of the photo feed and the short videos of this moment, and not as a photo mode on its own.

The same phone carries the radio outside the car, and the promised podcasts. Which other apps are fixed on the home screen has not been published as a list yet.
