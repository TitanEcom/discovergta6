Gameplay

Switching between Jason and Lucia

You play as either of them, together or each on your own. No shared screen with a second player at launch.

The game is played in third person, as Jason or as Lucia. After the Extended Look, Rockstar described that you can split them: hang out together, or head into the city separately.

Launch is a single-player experience. An online mode in the vein of GTA Online is not part of the November 19, 2026 announcement.
