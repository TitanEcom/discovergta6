Relationship

The bond between Jason and Lucia responds to how you play, and it is not mandatory romance.

Trailers sell VI as a love story and a crime story. In the August 2026 preview, Rockstar put a caveat on that: the relationship is optional.

Safehouses spread across Leonida are compared in the press to the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2, as places where the conversation between the two can land. How much weight that system carries is something we learn only in the game.
