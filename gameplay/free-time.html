Gameplay

Free time

Diving, skydiving, wrestling, basketball, and working out are in the previews.

Alongside shooting, stealing, and driving away from the police, the Extended Look showed skydiving, wrestling, basketball, working out, and diving. Fishing is mentioned separately with the Squalo.

Trailers show a much wider fleet than the seven vehicles that received a name in the editions, on land, on water, and in the air. Weapons on screen got engravings, tints, and skins. Rockstar publishes a full list of those only with the game.
