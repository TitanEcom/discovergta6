Gameplay

Body, sleep, and appearance

Weight, exhaustion, and a wardrobe you put together yourself.

Both leads can get heavier or lighter. Stay awake for days on end and it shows in the face. Working out is on the leisure list from the Extended Look.

Hair, clothes, and accessories can be changed. An experiment that made you buy groceries and stock the fridge was cut by Rockstar, because it got in the way of the game's flow.
