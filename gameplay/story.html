Story of Jason and Lucia

A crime story about two partners in Leonida, after a job that looked simple gets out of hand.

Grand Theft Auto VI follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Both already know the rough side of life before they come to need each other. A job that was supposed to stay simple lands them in the middle of a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida.

It is the first main game in the series whose script was not written by Dan Houser. Rupert Humphries and Michael Wiafe are named as the writers. In interviews, the studio describes the relationship as one in which both characters are allowed to be vulnerable, tough, and fallible.

The marketing leans hard on the couple. After the August 2026 Extended Look, Rockstar also told the trade press that the relationship is optional for the player: you can stick together, or each go your own way.

Rockstar's official summary sets the tone: Jason and Lucia 'have always known the deck is stacked against them.' When an easy score goes wrong, they end up 'on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America,' caught in a conspiracy that stretches across Leonida and forced to rely on each other more than ever.

The supporting cast pulls the story in several directions at once. Brian Heder and Cal Hampton belong to Jason's smuggling life in the Keys. Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, and Real Dimez bring in Vice City's music and club scene. Raul Bautista is a bank robber who recruits people willing to take big risks. Writer Rupert Humphries has described the setup as a Bonnie and Clyde-style story. According to The New York Times, producer Rob Nelson's playthrough took about 80 hours, counting the main story and optional goals with story consequences.
