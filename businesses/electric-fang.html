Electric Fang

A tattoo shop in Stockyard, tied to the Ultimate Edition.

Electric Fang is the tattoo shop from the Ultimate list. Tattoos for Jason and Lucia are also in the broader style set of that edition.

The official text: 'Stockyard's most iconic ink bar, with over 50 signature tattoos for both Jason and Lucia, all designed by the artist collective FAILE. Only open for business with the Ultimate Edition.' FAILE is a Brooklyn art duo known for street art and murals, which fits Stockyard's mural theme.
