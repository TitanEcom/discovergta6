One-Eyed Willie's

Off-road mods and painted cars at Lake Leonida.

One-Eyed Willie's is the workshop for off-road modifications and hand-painted art, placed at Lake Leonida in the edition guides that follow the Rockstar list.

The official text: 'This mod shop in Lake Leonida specializes in off-road modifications and hand-painted automotive artistry. Only open for business with the Ultimate Edition.' It pairs naturally with off-road vehicles such as the Dominator Buggy.
