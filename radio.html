Muziek

Radio

On October 8, 2026, Rockstar put six stations online as clips. For the first time the radio keeps playing when you leave the car, through earbuds. Podcasts are promised and still unnamed. The score is by Justice. Alongside that comes GTA VI: The Album, with 34 original songs.

Radio · Preview Flash FM The returning pop station, now with Robyn and Alex Consani. Radio · Preview Cocoteo FM A Latin station with the on-air names El Martillo and La Gata. Radio · Preview Afro Bank FM Amapiano, African classics, and 3-step. Radio · Preview The Chamber 106.6 Metal from the 1980s onward, with DJ Kerry and DJ Tom. Radio · Preview Dirty South Classics Regional rap and hip-hop classics, with Trick and Trina. Radio · Preview Back Country Radio Country from the swamp, with MW, Delta Dawne, and an airboat weatherman.

Not in the preview

Reference works name V-Rock, Emotion 98.3, and Vice City FM as returning titles from earlier Vice City games. They were not in the six-station preview. Until Rockstar broadcasts them, they are names from the series’ history, not a confirmed VI playlist.
