Leonida

Locations

No drawn map. Only places named in an official announcement or edition text. Rockstar has not published distances between them.

Location · Confirmed Leonida The fictional state where the whole game is set, built as a satire of Florida in the 2020s. Location · Confirmed Vice City The coastal city returns, this time as Leonida's Miami. Location · Confirmed Leonida Keys The state's island group, modeled on the Florida Keys, and Jason's working ground. Location · Confirmed Grassrivers The watery interior, built after the Everglades. Location · Confirmed Port Gellhorn A named place outside the core of Vice City. Location · Confirmed Ambrosia One of Leonida's named regions, outside the city of Vice City. Location · Confirmed Mount Kalaga National Park A national park, and the terrain the Dominator buggy was built for. Location · Confirmed Leonida Penitentiary The prison where Lucia was held before the story begins. Location · Edition Shore Drive The coastal road the '95 Grotti Cheetah is an ode to. Location · Edition Washington Beach The beach where the Ultimate Edition's Shitzu Squalo is moored. Location · Edition Gambit Bay A bay Rockstar names as fishing water for the Squalo. Location · Edition Watson Bay The neighborhood of Paradise Garage. Location · Edition Stockyard An urban district with fashion, tattoos, and murals made by street artists. Location · Edition Southside Vice City The part of the city where the PTT Youngin$ compound stands. Location · Edition Lake Leonida The lake where One-Eyed Willie's off-road garage is said to stand. Location · Background Liberty City The old city from earlier games. In VI, the past of Lucia's family, and not announced as a playable area.

Businesses

Clubs, labels, garages, and shops that already have a name. Some open only with the Ultimate Edition.

Business · Confirmed Only Raw Records The record label of Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan Priest. Business · Confirmed Jack of Hearts The strip club in Boobie Ike's empire. Business · Confirmed Brian's Boatyard The yard where Brian Heder still moves product. Business · Edition Paradise Garage Storage for the Dominator buggy, with a gun locker and a spot to fence goods. Business · Edition The Stanier's Garage The garage that comes with the '55 Stanier. Business · Edition Stock 305 A streetwear shop in Stockyard, only with the Ultimate Edition. Business · Edition Sara's Unisex Salon A salon for hair, beard, makeup, and nails in Stockyard. Business · Edition Electric Fang A tattoo shop in Stockyard, tied to the Ultimate Edition. Business · Edition Rideout Customs A workshop for interiors, rims, and donk styling. Business · Edition One-Eyed Willie's Off-road mods and painted cars at Lake Leonida. Business · Edition PTT Youngin$ A gang compound. The raid on it is Ultimate content.
