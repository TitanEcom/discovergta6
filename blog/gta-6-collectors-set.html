The $400 GTA 6 Collector's Set has no game in it

Rockstar's Collector's Set is a box of merchandise built around Macca the Gator. It costs $399.99, and the game is sold separately.

November 5, 2026 · 2 min read · By DiscoverGTA6

Price, date, and the catch

Rockstar revealed the GTA 6 Collector's Set in September. It costs $399.99 on the Rockstar Store, ships for November 19, and is limited to one order per person while supplies last. It does not include a copy of the game. It is meant as a companion to any version, digital or boxed.

What is in the box

Coverage lists eleven items, most of them themed around Macca the Gator, the in-game TV character:

  • A Macca the Gator figure with a stash compartment
  • Matte black GTA VI Oakley Frogskins sunglasses
  • A New Era 9FORTY snapback with Vice City details
  • A crossbody bag and a shot glass
  • A magnetic mirror, a swizzle spoon, and a razor blade keychain
  • An enamel pin set, stickers, and a map poster

Some of the items lean into the game's satire of excess, which made the set a talking point in September.

What it really costs

Add a Standard Edition at $79.99 and you are near $480 before tax. With the Ultimate Edition, it is closer to $500.

Has Rockstar done this before?

Yes. Red Dead Redemption 2 also had a collector's box without the game. If you want in-game extras rather than physical ones, the Ultimate Edition is the place to look. Compare both on our editions page.

Sources: Insider Gaming, Consequence, GamingBible, Boing Boing. Spotted a mistake? Let us know.
