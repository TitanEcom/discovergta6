Standard or Ultimate? Picking your GTA 6 edition before launch

Both editions contain the full game. The difference is $20 worth of extras, and one deadline that applies to both.

November 8, 2026 · 2 min read · By DiscoverGTA6

What both editions share

The Standard Edition ($79.99) is the complete game. The Ultimate Edition ($99.99) is the same game plus extras. Neither gives faster progression or better missions.

What the extra $20 buys

  • Vehicles: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the Shitzu Squalo boat, the '67 Dominator Buggy with Paradise Garage, Jason's Dinka Enduro and kayak, and a Ganado rebuild.
  • Weapons: the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers and engraved pistols for Jason and Lucia.
  • Shops: Stock 305, Electric Fang, Sara's Unisex Salon, Rideout Customs, and One-Eyed Willie's.
  • Side content: the PTT Youngin$ compound raid and the Classic Car Collection commission.

Rockstar says the extras are spread across the story, not handed out on day one.

Most buyers picked Ultimate

Sensor Tower estimated in August that 89% of pre-orders were for the Ultimate Edition. That is a market estimate, not a Rockstar number.

The deadline that matters

Buy either edition digitally before November 20 and you get the Vintage Vice City Pack: the '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage, retro outfits, a weapon pattern, and a month of GTA+. Physical copies are a code in a box, with no disc.

Which one should you get?

If you want more cars, shops, and outfits woven into the story, Ultimate is the better deal per dollar. If you only care about the story and the open world, Standard gives you all of it. Either way, buy before November 20 to keep the pack. Compare everything on our editions page.

Sources: Insider Gaming: Ultimate Edition, GAMES.GG: editions explained, Wikipedia: Grand Theft Auto VI. Spotted a mistake? Let us know.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Ultimate Edition give a gameplay advantage?

No. It adds vehicles, weapons, shops, outfits, and side content, but no faster progression.

When is the pre-order bonus deadline?

Buy digitally before November 20, 2026 to get the Vintage Vice City Pack with either edition.
