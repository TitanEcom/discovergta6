Six things the Extended Look taught us about GTA 6

Rockstar's 26-minute Extended Look in August is still the best view of how GTA 6 plays. These are the six changes that matter most.

November 7, 2026 · 2 min read · By DiscoverGTA6

1. You switch between Jason and Lucia

Character switching from GTA V returns. In missions, the two can handle different parts of the same job, and in one escape Lucia drives while Jason fires at the police. More on our switching page.

2. The Wanted level goes to six stars

Police work from descriptions of your appearance, clothes, and car. A crime has to be reported first, and hollow stars mean they have not identified you yet. A mask or a change of clothes can help you disappear.

3. A reputation that sticks

The new Criminal Profile tracks how you work, a bit like honor in Red Dead Redemption 2. Unlike the Wanted level, it does not reset when the police lose you.

4. Your body shows your habits

Fast food and drinking add weight, working out builds muscle, and days without sleep show in the face. See our body and sleep page.

5. A long list of things to do

The footage showed basketball, pool, weightlifting, scuba diving, kayaking, fishing, hunting, base jumping, races, jet skis, and fanboats. The full list is on our activities page.

6. The relationship is up to you

Jason and Lucia can be a couple or platonic partners. Time spent together brings them closer, and coverage says the strength of the bond affects how the story plays out. Read more on our relationship page.

Sources: Red Bull, KeenGamer, Wikipedia: Grand Theft Auto VI. Spotted a mistake? Let us know.
