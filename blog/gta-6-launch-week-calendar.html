GTA 6 launch week: every date from November 12 to December 11

Tomorrow the download opens. Here is every date worth knowing between now and the Justice score in December.

November 11, 2026 · 2 min read · By DiscoverGTA6

Thursday, November 12: preload

Digital pre-orders and boxed codes can start downloading. Read our preload guide.

Wednesday, November 18: A Night in Vice City

The Miami Heat turn Kaseya Center into Vice City for a game-night event the evening before launch. Background in our Miami post.

Thursday, November 19: release day

GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The same day, GTA VI: The Album comes out, and the $399.99 Collector's Set ships.

Before Friday, November 20: the bonus deadline

Buy digitally before November 20 to get the Vintage Vice City Pack with the '55 Stanier and a month of GTA+.

Friday, December 11: the Justice score

The original score by Justice, 30 tracks, arrives on streaming, CD, and vinyl. More in our score post.

Early 2027: PC, maybe

A report points to January to March 2027 for a PC version. Rockstar has not confirmed it.

See you in Leonida. Keep an eye on the countdown and the blog for launch-day updates.

Sources: Forbes: GTA VI preload window, NBC Miami, Rockstar Newswire: Original Score by Justice, Insider Gaming: Collector's Set. Spotted a mistake? Let us know.
