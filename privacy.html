Privacy

Effective October 25, 2026. In short: there is no account, no analytics cookie, and no server that receives your message.

1. Who

GTA Wiki is a fan project with no company behind it. There is no processor in the sense of an editorial desk that receives mail. For questions use the contact form, knowing the message stays on your own device.

2. What we do not collect

The site sets no tracking cookies, no ads, and no analytics of its own. Pages are static files. A visit sends only what a browser always sends to a host when the site is online, such as an IP address in that host’s log. If you open the files locally, that visit does not leave your computer.

3. Local storage

Two keys in localStorage, only if you use the matching feature:

  • gtawiki-mail: name, email, subject, and text from the contact form, plus a ticket number and time.
  • gtawiki-recent: the ids of the last entries you opened.

That data does not go to a backend. Anyone with access to this browser profile can read it. Clear it on the contact page, or through the site data in your browser. It is kept until you delete it.

4. Fonts and video

Big Shoulders Display and Outfit load from this site, under the SIL Open Font License. That request does not go to Google. Trailer playback and stills load from YouTube, which has its own privacy rules.

5. Sharing

We do not sell data, because we do not receive it. A link to Rockstar or a store falls under that site’s privacy rules.

6. Children

The site is not for children. Do not send a minor’s details through the form.

7. Your choices

You do not have to use the form. You can block local storage in the browser. The countdown and the archive still work. The inbox and recently viewed list do not.

8. Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
