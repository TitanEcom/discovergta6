Privacy

Effective November 2, 2026. In short: there are no accounts, but this site does use Google Analytics, Google AdSense advertising, and Vercel Web Analytics.

1. Who

Discover GTA 6 (discovergta6.com) is an independent fan project. For questions about this notice, use the contact page.

2. Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives technical data with each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page you request, and may keep it in short-lived server logs for security and operation.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read and how visitors find the site. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and collects data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived. We see this only as aggregated statistics. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, and, where allowed, personalize them based on your visits to this and other websites. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can turn off personalized ads in Google’s ad settings or through aboutads.info.

5. Vercel Web Analytics

We also use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. It records a few anonymous events: opening the search, the search term and the result you click, archive filters, links you follow to other sites (the domain only), and the subject category of the contact form. It does not record your name, email, or message.

6. Local storage

One key in your browser’s localStorage: gtawiki-recent, the ids of the last entries you opened, used for the “recently viewed” list. It is not sent to us. Clear it through the site data in your browser.

The contact form does not store or send your message itself. It opens your own email app with the message filled in, and you decide whether to send it. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

7. Fonts and video

Fonts load from this site, not from Google Fonts. Trailers and stills load from YouTube, and images load from Rockstar Games’ servers. Those services receive your IP address and have their own privacy rules.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The services above (Vercel, Google, YouTube, Rockstar) receive data directly from your browser as described. A link to Rockstar or a store falls under that site’s privacy rules.

9. Your rights and choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, use the opt-outs above, or use the site without the contact form. If you live in the EU, the EEA, or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, and objection, and you may complain to your data protection authority. Because we only see aggregated statistics, requests about analytics or advertising data are best made to Google directly.

10. Children

The site is not for children. Grand Theft Auto VI is rated for adults. Do not send a minor’s details through the form.

11. Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Frequently asked questions

Does Discover GTA 6 use cookies?

Yes. Google Analytics and Google AdSense set cookies. Vercel Web Analytics works without cookies.

Can I opt out of personalized ads?

Yes, through Google's ad settings or aboutads.info. The links are in the advertising section above.
