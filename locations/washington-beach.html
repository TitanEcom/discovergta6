Washington Beach

The beach where the Ultimate Edition's Shitzu Squalo is moored.

The pink-and-blue Shitzu Squalo from the Ultimate Edition is moored at Washington Beach. The boat is tied to fishing on Gambit Bay, and it has a crate of explosives on board.
