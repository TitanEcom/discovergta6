Locations

Stockyard

An urban district with fashion, tattoos, and murals made by street artists.

Stockyard is the district Rockstar tied to mural art. More than fifty street artists worked on walls in the city, with this neighborhood as one of the places named. The real-world model is the Wynwood art district.

The Ultimate Edition places Stock 305, Sara's salon, and Electric Fang, a tattoo shop, here. Those three open only with that edition.
