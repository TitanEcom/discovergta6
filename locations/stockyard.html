Stockyard

An urban district with fashion, tattoos, and murals made by street artists.

Stockyard is the district Rockstar tied to mural art. More than fifty street artists worked on walls in the game, with this neighborhood as one of the places named. The real-world model is Miami's Wynwood art district.

Two Ultimate Edition shops are placed here by name. Stock 305 is described as 'Stockyard's premiere destination for elevated streetwear,' and Electric Fang as 'Stockyard's most iconic ink bar,' with more than 50 tattoos for Jason and Lucia designed by the artist collective FAILE. Both open only with the Ultimate Edition.

Guides put Stockyard inside Vice City. Rockstar has not published a map that shows where it lies.
