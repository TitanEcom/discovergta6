Locations

Leonida

The fictional state where the whole game is set, built as a satire of Florida in the 2020s.

Leonida is the series' new state, and the full playable world Rockstar has announced so far. Vice City, previously the setting of Vice City and Vice City Stories, sits inside it and is once again the heart of the story.

The state parodies America in the 2020s: social media, influencer culture, police bodycams, hurricanes, tourism, and the chaos known outside the state as Florida Man. A Rockstar research team worked out of Miami.

After the Extended Look, the press talked about a map that would be a multiple of the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar has not published that ratio as an official figure. Neighborhoods and county names that circulate only in leaks are not treated as fact here.
