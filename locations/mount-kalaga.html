Mount Kalaga National Park

A national park, and the terrain the Dominator buggy was built for.

Mount Kalaga National Park is the state's rugged wilderness. In the Ultimate Edition, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy is presented as a mud machine for the forests of this park and the land around it.

Rockstar's region text, titled 'Wild, Wild Country,' describes a national landmark up against the state's northern border, with room to breathe and prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails. It is also where 'hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals' live far from the prying eyes of the government.

Official screenshots show forested ridges, and the Extended Look includes kayaking in the park. Fan maps compare its canyons to the hill country of northern Florida and southern Georgia.
