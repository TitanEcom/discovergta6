Port Gellhorn

A named place outside the core of Vice City.

Port Gellhorn is one of the places Rockstar has named alongside Vice City, the Keys, Grassrivers, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga. A neighborhood guide of its own has not appeared yet.

Until the studio publishes maps with counties, this archive treats Port Gellhorn as a confirmed place name, and not as a drawn map.
