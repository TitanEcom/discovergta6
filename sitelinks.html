More from us

Sitelinks

Discover GTA 6 is not our only project. Each site below has its own topic, from a Rust wiki to live Dutch emergency alerts and steering wheel covers. Here is what you find on each one, and who it is for.

112melder.nl: live P2000 alerts from the Netherlands

Ever wondered why an ambulance just raced down your street? 112melder.nl shows every P2000 alert in the Netherlands live, in one place. Ambulance, fire brigade, and police, updated the moment a call goes out. Each alert lists the time, the priority, the service, and the town.

Pick your own town or province and you only see what happens near you. There is also an overview per safety region and an explanation of how the emergency services work. The site is in Dutch.

hardwarevergelijken.nl: graphics cards and processors side by side

Building a PC that runs the next big open-world game smoothly? hardwarevergelijken.nl puts GPUs and CPUs side by side, with current performance figures and clear advice. You get rankings of the fastest models with a score, and you can compare two models head to head, such as an RTX 4070 against an RX 7800 XT.

That saves digging through separate reviews when you build a new rig or upgrade an old one. You see at a glance how much faster one model is than the other. The site is in Dutch.

randomsoundbuttons.com: sound effects in one click

Need a sound effect for a clip, a stream, or a prank? randomsoundbuttons.com is a soundboard full of memes, game sounds, reactions, troll sounds, and classics. Press a button and the sound plays right away.

Every sound has its own page where you can like, share, and download it. The buttons are grouped into Trending, New, Memes, Games, and Reactions, so you find what you need fast. Nothing to install.

rustbench.com: wiki and calculators for Rust

Play the survival game Rust? rustbench.com is a free wiki with tools and calculators. For every item you see the crafting recipe, what it gives in the recycler, and where to find it. According to the site, it covers more than 1,200 items.

The raid calculator shows what it costs to break a wall or a door, and the recycler tool shows what you get back. You also find CCTV codes and guides to the monuments. The makers update the site after every patch.

ikbereken.nl: free calculators for Dutch money questions

Living or working in the Netherlands? ikbereken.nl has free calculators for everyday money questions: gross to net salary, maximum mortgage, VAT, holiday pay, and allowances such as healthcare and rent benefit. The tools use the current Dutch rates, with the official source listed.

Every tool explains how the result is worked out, with a worked example and the assumptions behind it. The amounts you fill in stay in your browser. The site is in Dutch.

zoekdirect.nl: simple search

zoekdirect.nl is a calm Dutch search page. Type your question and search straight away, without distractions. Shortcut buttons take you to the weather, news, a route, or a translator, and you get suggestions such as train times or supermarket opening hours.

Sites you visit through zoekdirect show up under "Recently visited", so a site you use often is one click away. Handy as the start page of your browser.

covermywheel.com: custom steering wheel covers

Is the leather on your steering wheel worn or peeling? covermywheel.com makes custom steering wheel covers and wraps. Each cover is cut for your make and model, and you fit it yourself with a step-by-step guide. No need to take your car to an upholsterer.

According to the shop, you get your money back if the cover does not fit. There is a 2-year warranty on material defects, and they ship worldwide with tracking.
