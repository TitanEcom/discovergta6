GTA VI: The Album

An album of 34 original songs, alongside the score by Justice.

On September 17, 2026, Rockstar announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, with 34 original songs. The music press names Travis Scott, Rauw Alejandro, and Keith Richards among the names around that album. The full tracklist stays out of this summary until it is officially settled. The score for the trailers and the game is a separate thread: Justice.

IGN reported that the album also gets a physical CD and vinyl release. It is set to come out on November 19, 2026, the same day as the game.

Singles out before release include Travis Scott's RHYNO, Morgan Wallen's Last Thing You Need, Rauw Alejandro's Macacoa 2000, and Justice's Flower in a Hurricane with Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Whether album songs also play on the in-game radio has not been confirmed.
