Cover and editions

Standard and Ultimate become official, with the Vintage pack as the pre-order.

On June 18, 2026, Rockstar showed the cover and the two editions. Ultimate adds clothes, hairstyles, places, missions, tattoos, vehicles, and weapons. Pre-orders get the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Ultimate Edition contents and the pre-order bonuses were confirmed on June 24, 2026, the day before pre-orders opened. The same week it became clear that physical copies would be a code in a box, without a disc, and some retailers said they would not stock it.

Prices: $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US. A separate collector's set without the game followed in September 2026.
