Preload and the box with a code

From local midnight you can download. The physical edition is a code in a box.

Digital pre-orders can start downloading on November 12 at local midnight. The physical version is in stores from that day and contains a code, not a disc, so the same preload is possible. The game itself starts on November 19.

The physical box was confirmed in June 2026: both editions ship as a download code, with no disc. Some retailers said publicly that they would not stock it.

The Vintage Vice City Pack depends on a timely digital purchase, before November 20, 2026.
