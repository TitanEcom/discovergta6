Six radio stations in preview

Rockstar puts six stations online, with clips and hosts.

On October 8, 2026, Rockstar published the first six stations: Flash FM, Cocoteo FM, Afro Bank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Dirty South Classics, and Back Country Radio. The studio promises the widest radio and podcasts to date. Older names such as V-Rock, Emotion 98.3, and Vice City FM are cited in reference works as returning, but they were not in this preview.

Rockstar published the stations on its official music page as previews of about two minutes each, with three tracks per station. For the first time, radio and a series of on-demand podcasts can be heard on foot as well, through headphones and earbuds connected to Jason and Lucia's phones.

The six stations: pop on Flash FM, modern Latin on Cocoteo FM, African music on Afro Bank FM, metal on The Chamber 106.6, southern rap on Dirty South Classics, and country on Back Country Radio. Rockstar says the full radio spans everything from classic rock to talk radio.
