Launch trailer

The last big trailer before release. Justice provides the score.

The launch trailer arrived on October 15, 2026. The song is Flower in a Hurricane by Justice, with Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. That also made it known that Justice is writing the game's original score.

The trailer's song also appears on GTA VI: The Album. Justice's role covers both: they wrote the game's original score, and Flower in a Hurricane is one of the album's singles.

It was the last big trailer before preload on November 12 and release on November 19.
