Flash FM

The returning pop station, now with Robyn and Alex Consani.

Flash FM is a name from Vice City, and it returns as a pop station. Rockstar names Robyn and Alex Consani as the voices.

The official preview included, among others, Charli xcx with Good Ones, Zara Larsson and PinkPantheress with Midnight Sun (Girls Trip), and MGMT with Electric Feel. The preview plays clips, not full songs.

For the first time in the series, the radio does not stop when you get out. Through earbuds on the phone, you carry it through the city. Podcasts have been promised on that phone; titles have not been named yet.

Rockstar calls Flash FM 'the Vice City institution that delivers the best in pop music,' guaranteed to make you dance. The station first played in the 2002 game Grand Theft Auto: Vice City and returned in Vice City Stories.

The six previewed stations are not the full lineup. Rockstar says the radio spans 'everything from Classic Rock to Talk Radio,' and neither format is among the six.
