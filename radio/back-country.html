Back Country Radio

Country from the swamp, with MW, Delta Dawne, and an airboat weatherman.

Back Country Radio is the country station, written for airboats and the interior. MW and Delta Dawne are the names Rockstar itself uses. Mud Dog Mike delivers the weather report from the water.

Reference works link the hosting to Lana Del Rey and Morgan Wallen. Because Rockstar names the characters in the preview and does not use the civilian name everywhere, both layers are kept here.

Clips: David Allan Coe with Need a Little Time Off for Bad Behavior, Loretta Lynn with Don't Come Home A-Drinkin', and Hank Williams with The Conversation.

Rockstar's copy brings 'renegade country music' to the Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, and beyond. Mud Dog Mike is introduced as a roving reporter and swamp meteorologist, and as Delta Dawne's 'not-so-secret lover.'

Full titles from the preview: Loretta Lynn's Don't Come Home A-Drinkin' (With Lovin' On Your Mind), and The Conversation, a duet by Hank Williams Jr. and Waylon Jennings.
