Dirty South Classics

Regional rap and hip-hop classics, with Trick and Trina.

Dirty South Classics is the station for southern rap. Trick and Trina are the hosts; the announcement is read as Trick Daddy and Trina.

Clips: David Banner with Like A Pimp, JT Money with Solé on Who Dat, and DJ Khaled with Trick Daddy, Rick Ross, and Pitbull on Born N Raised.
