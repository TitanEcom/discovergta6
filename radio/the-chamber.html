The Chamber 106.6

Metal from the 1980s onward, with DJ Kerry and DJ Tom.

The Chamber 106.6 is the metal station. DJ Kerry and DJ Tom host it. The press links them to Kerry King and Tom Araya of Slayer.

The preview included Black Sabbath with I, Pantera with I'm Broken, and Slayer with Raining Blood.

Rockstar's copy opens with 'Crushing metal has landed in Leonida,' and promises classic metal from the '80s and beyond. In 2002's Vice City, V-Rock filled that slot. Rockstar has not said whether V-Rock returns.

Rockstar has not confirmed who voices DJ Kerry and DJ Tom. The link to Slayer comes from the press.
