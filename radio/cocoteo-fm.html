Cocoteo FM

A Latin station with the on-air names El Martillo and La Gata.

Cocoteo FM is the Latin station from the first preview. The tagline that comes with it is a party that never burns out. El Martillo and La Gata are the hosts; the music press and reference works link those roles to Bad Bunny and RaiNao.

Clips in the preview: Bad Bunny with EoO, Axel Rulay with Si Es Trucho (Remix), and Romeo Santos with Canalla.

Rockstar's copy calls it 'Leonida's home for the best of modern Latin music,' from bachata and merengue to dembow and reggaeton, with the tagline 'el party que nunca se apaga.'

Full credits on the preview: Axel Rulay's Si Es Trucho Es Trucho (Remix) features Farruko and El Alfa, and Canalla is Romeo Santos with El Chaval de la Bachata.
