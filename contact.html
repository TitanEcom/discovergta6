Contact Discover GTA 6

Questions, corrections, tips, or press requests? Send us a message with the form below. We read every message.

What we can help with

  • Corrections. Spotted a mistake? Tell us which page and what is wrong, ideally with a source. We check it and update the entry once the change is confirmed.
  • Missing entries. A character, place, or vehicle Rockstar has named that is not on the site yet.
  • Questions about the site. Including advertising, privacy, and how entries are sourced.
  • Press and partnerships. Requests about Discover GTA 6 itself.

Not for game support. Problems with an order, a download code, or the game itself go to Rockstar Support. Discover GTA 6 is an independent fan site and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

The form opens your own email app with the message ready to send. You can also write to contact@discovergta6.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I report a mistake?

Use the form on this page, or the "Send a correction" link on any wiki page. Tell us which page and what is wrong, ideally with a source.

Can you help with my GTA 6 order or download code?

No. Orders, codes, and game problems go to Rockstar Support.

Do you sell GTA 6?

No. Discover GTA 6 does not sell games or codes.
