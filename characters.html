Cast

GTA 6 Characters

Two playable leads, and the people Rockstar has already given a profile or a name. No biographies filled in from leaks.

Character · Confirmed Jason Duval Playable lead. A former soldier who works for smugglers in the Leonida Keys. Character · Confirmed Lucia Caminos Playable lead. Just out of Leonida Penitentiary, and the first female protagonist in the series who is not optional. Character · Confirmed Cal Hampton A paranoid friend of Jason's, obsessed with conspiracies and tied to Brian Heder. Character · Confirmed Boobie Ike A local legend who built an empire out of real estate, a strip club, and a studio. Character · Confirmed Brian Heder An old smuggler in the Keys, with a boatyard, a third wife, and a room for Jason. Character · Mentioned Lori Brian Heder's third wife. Mentioned only, still without a profile of her own. Character · Confirmed Dre'Quan Priest An ambitious music boss, and the other half of Only Raw Records. Character · Confirmed Real Dimez Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to Only Raw Records. Character · Confirmed Bae-Luxe One of the two rappers in Real Dimez. Character · Confirmed Roxy One of the two rappers in Real Dimez. Character · Confirmed Raul Bautista An experienced bank robber who looks for crews willing to take on riskier work. Character · Edition Macca the Gator A cartoon character from a hit series in the state, and the mascot of an Ultimate clothing line.
